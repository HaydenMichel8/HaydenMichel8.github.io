During the Make OH/O 2026 Makeathon my team participated in the intel challenge. The task was to develop a prototype to reduce water waste at semiconductor manufacturing plants. We researched the processes and identified a few places where water could be conserved and built a prototype to sort them accordingly. For example, some water had to be extremely clean to use for wafer processing to minimize impurities, but after it was used to clean it was discarded. We concluded that some of this water could be re-used for machine cooling if properly routed.

Our prototype used an arduino with a number of sensors to categorize the water that was placed in its 3D printed vessel. It would detect the temperature of the water as well as the cleanliness of it, based on factors like turbidity or particle concentration, and then turn the vessel and release a hatch to pour it into the correct output tank. The prototype worked well and it was cool to see the whole project come together into a working final product in only 24 hours.
